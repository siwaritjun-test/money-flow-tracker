Resilience Screener

Resilience score (0–100, ranked within each sector) = downside capture 25% · relative drawdown 20% · sector-adjusted alpha 20% · RS line vs its 1-year high 15% · up/down volume 10% · bounce since the sector's low 10%. Method. A strong stock in a bear market is one that holds up better than its own industry, not just better than the S&P 500 — otherwise every utility looks like a leader when tech sells off. For each sector ETF the page finds a stress window (its current decline if it is ≥10% off its 1-year high, otherwise its deepest peak-to-trough decline of the year) and measures every member stock over the same dates: downside/upside capture (mean stock return on the sector's down/up days ÷ the sector's), drawdown relative to the sector's, sector-adjusted alpha and beta (least squares on daily returns), the stock ÷ sector relative-strength line, up-day vs down-day volume, and the bounce off the sector's low. The four types split on capture ratios at 1.0, so Defensive names (low beta) are kept apart from true Leaders (fall less and rise more). Scores are percentile-ranked within each sector. Returns use dividend/split-adjusted closes over the last year of Yahoo Finance daily bars. Educational tool, not investment advice.
