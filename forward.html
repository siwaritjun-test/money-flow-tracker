Backup

Forward tests are saved in this browser only (like the ★ watchlist). Export a file to keep a backup or to move them to another browser or phone; importing merges and never overwrites.
Returns are dividend-adjusted and measured from the price shown when a position was added, so they include that session's move after the click. Prices come from the same daily data as the Stock Picker (updated by the repo's GitHub Action); tickers outside the S&P 500 are fetched live from Yahoo. A paper test — no commissions, slippage or taxes. Educational tool, not investment advice.
